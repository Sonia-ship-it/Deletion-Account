Privacy Policy

Request Account Deletion

Manage your privacy preferences. You can request the deletion of your Kwizera Shop account and its associated personal data at any time.

✉️

How to Request Deletion

Follow these simple steps to request the permanent deletion of your account:

  1. 1

    Send a deletion request email to:

    support@kwizerashop.com
  2. 2

    Include in the email body:

    • Your registered username / email address
    • An explicit request for account deletion
  3. 3

    Our support team will verify your ownership of the account.

  4. 4

    Your account and eligible personal data will be deleted after successful verification.

🛡️

Verification Process

To ensure security and prevent unauthorized requests, we perform the following steps:

  • Identity Verification: We may request confirmation or ask verifying questions before proceeding with the deletion. This prevents malicious third parties from requesting unauthorized deletions.
  • Secure Confirmation: Once the identity is verified and deletion is complete, you will receive a confirmation email at your registered email address.
  • Irreversible Process: Please note that once verification is complete and the deletion process starts, the account and associated details cannot be recovered.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon approval and verification, the following information will be permanently removed from our databases:

  • User account information Your unique user identifiers, roles, and administrative flags.
  • Login credentials Secure password hashes, session tokens, and keys.
  • User profile information Your full name, display picture, and associated metadata.
  • Personal information connected to the account Any directly identifiable records that connect you to the Kwizera Shop platform.
  • Authentication records Access histories, audit logs for login attempts, and device session keys.
📋

Data That May Be Retained

To comply with operational and administrative requirements, certain information may be retained when required for:

  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with applicable local laws and regulations.
  • Business Record Keeping: Maintaining the integrity of audit logs for general shop activities.
  • Financial Transaction Records: Essential records showing payment actions, invoices, or transactions which cannot be deleted for tax and audit compliance.
  • Fraud Prevention: Critical session indicators or account tracking necessary to prevent abuse.
  • Regulatory Requirements: Meeting record-keeping standards set by business regulators.
Retention Period:

Some business records may be retained for the period required by applicable laws and business requirements.
